# Changelog

All notable changes to the Awesome AI Agent Stack are documented here.

## [2026.11.16] - 2026-11-16

### Added

- 5 tools carried over from the backup repository: sandeco/reversa, Wan-Video/Wan2.2, shiyu-coder/Kronos, Fincept-Corporation/FinceptTerminal and isair/jarvis.
- `scripts/audit/run.py`, the audit script behind the mechanical checks.

- 21 links updated to follow repositories that were renamed or moved to a new owner.

- 55 tools that no longer pass the bar, whatever their star count: 39 with no push in 12 months, 14 archived and 2 forks.

- 10 duplicate lines: 9 old names of repositories already listed under their new name, and 1 repeated entry.
